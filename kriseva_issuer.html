Capability Sketches

What issuer-side intelligence could look like.

Each card describes a capability under validation with senior issuer-side stakeholders. Wording is exploratory by design.

01

AI-assisted bid evaluation

Operator-assisted workflows for technical and financial bid evaluation. The evaluator stays in the loop; the system surfaces the evidence.

Under exploration

02

RFP ambiguity surfacing

Pre-publication review of RFP language for ambiguity and contradiction. Reduces clarification volume and bidder confusion.

Under exploration

03

Compliance review burden

Compliance evidence presented per bidder, anchored to the source clause. Reduces reconstruction time without removing the reviewer.

Under exploration

04

Evidence-linked evaluation

Every evaluation note points back to a clause and a page in the bid. The decision artifact is reviewable, including by audit.

Under exploration

05

Auditability

Decision history, evaluator override trail, and corrigenda watch live with the bundle, not in a separate spreadsheet.

Under exploration

06

Procurement-side decision traceability

The procurement-side decision artifact is structured the same way as the bidder-side one. Both sides see the same evidence shape.

Under exploration
