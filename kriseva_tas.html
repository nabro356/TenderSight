Product · KRISEVA TAS

The bidder-side
Tender Automation System.

KRISEVA TAS is the bidder-side tender automation and procurement intelligence product for defense MSMEs and defense-tech companies. It helps bid teams discover or ingest tender bundles, parse documents, extract structured requirements, score relevance against company capability context, organize compliance evidence, and generate BID / REVIEW / SKIP review language.

Status · in pilot / demo evaluation · not production SaaS

Source-Linked Extraction

Extraction that survives the audit.

Extracts key tender metadata such as buyer fields, dates, values, EMD/ePBG, eligibility signals, and document requirements. Each value carries a back-pointer to the bundle clause that produced it.

  • Buyer / agency fields — issuing entity, contracting officer, location, mode.
  • Dates — publication, pre-bid, submission, opening, validity, EMD release.
  • Values — estimated cost, EMD, ePBG, performance security, payment terms.
  • Eligibility signals — turnover, past performance, MSE/MII declarations, certifications.
  • Document requirements — what the bidder must furnish, by clause and annex.

Decision Brief

BID / REVIEW / SKIP — recommendations the operator reviews.

The brief is a recommendation, not an action. The operator reviews the rationale, accepts or overrides, and records the decision against the bundle.

BID

Eligibility clear, compliance signals favorable, fit aligned with company capability context.

Recommended · operator confirms

REVIEW

Material ambiguity, partial compliance gap, or missing certifications. Operator review required before submission.

Held · operator reviews evidence

SKIP

Eligibility miss, capacity gap, or risk threshold breached. Skip with a documented rationale.

Skipped · rationale recorded
