Before · After

The same operator, with one workflow change.

Manual portal-and-Excel discipline does the work but does not produce a reviewable artifact. TAS produces the artifact alongside the operator decision.

BEFORE TAS

Manual · scattered

  1. 01 Manual portal search across GeM, DefProc public, DPSU sites.
  2. 02 Scattered downloads — RFP, BOQ, ATCs, corrigenda each in a different folder.
  3. 03 RFP, spec, BOQ, corrigenda, annexures reviewed separately.
  4. 04 Excel tracking with manual status columns.
  5. 05 Technical fit decided over calls and chat threads.
  6. 06 Compliance reconstructed from memory and a tab-switching pass.
  7. 07 Bid / no-bid decisioning lives in a person, not a record.

The work is real, but it is not reviewable.

→

WITH TAS

Bundle · evidence-linked · operator-recorded

  1. 01 Supported import / search flows for GeM and DefProc public discovery.
  2. 02 Tender represented as a bundle, not isolated files.
  3. 03 Parsing status tracked per document, with retry where parse fails.
  4. 04 Extracted key clauses and fields with clause + page citations.
  5. 05 Relevance score against company capability context, override-able.
  6. 06 Compliance requirements with source evidence where available.
  7. 07 Decision brief with BID / REVIEW / SKIP recommendation.
  8. 08 Operator records the decision and rationale against the bundle.

Operator-shaped. Source-linked. Audit-ready.

Pipeline

Eight steps from alert to decision.

Each step is operator-visible and reviewable. The pipeline does not autonomously submit anything; it produces an evidence-linked recommendation the operator confirms or overrides.

01

Discover or ingest

GeM · DefProc public · upload

Live GeM discovery and DefProc public discovery, plus operator-assisted protected-document flows. Direct uploads and pasted bundles are first-class.
02

Bundle assembly

RFP · BOQ · ATC · corrigenda

Documents are grouped into a bundle. Corrigenda are tracked against the original tender so changes do not get lost.
03

Parse

PDF · DOCX · XLSX · TXT · CSV · OCR routing

Local parsing for text-bearing files. Scanned/low-text PDFs are routed through local OCR with quality clearly disclosed.
04

Extract key fields

metadata · dates · values · eligibility

Buyer fields, dates, EMD/ePBG, eligibility signals, and document requirements. Each value carries a clause + page back-pointer.
05

Score relevance

capability context

Relevance scoring against the company’s capability context. Operator overrides are first-class and recorded against the bundle.
06

Compile compliance

clause-anchored evidence

Compliance requirements are extracted and structured for human review. Gaps are flagged with the source clause that triggered them.
07

Generate decision brief

operator review brief

The brief packages relevance, compliance, and the BID / REVIEW / SKIP recommendation, with every claim back-linked to its evidence.
08

Operator decision

recorded against bundle

The operator confirms or overrides the recommendation, records the rationale, and the decision becomes part of the bundle’s history.
Evidence Trail

One trail, from clause to decision.

Every recommendation in the brief points back to a clause and a page. Reviewers, not summary paragraphs, decide.
