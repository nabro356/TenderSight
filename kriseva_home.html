Problem Thesis

Defense procurement is document intelligence, compliance, evaluation, and decision-traceability.

Across defense MSMEs and defense-tech bid teams, the operational bottleneck is not finding tenders. It is the work between a tender alert and a defensible BID / REVIEW / SKIP decision: scattered documents, manual eligibility cross-checks, compliance reconstruction, and decisions that need a source trail.

01

Document intelligence

Tenders ship as bundles of bid documents, specifications, BOQs, ATCs, corrigenda, and annexures — not single PDFs.

02

Compliance reconstruction

Eligibility, EMD/ePBG, certifications, and Make-in-India clauses live across pages and documents that have to be read together.

03

Evidence-linked evaluation

A defensible decision needs every claim to point back to a clause and a page, not a summary paragraph.

04

Decision traceability

BID / REVIEW / SKIP is a structured operator decision. The brief should be reviewable a quarter from now.
The Product · KRISEVA TAS

From tender bundle to decision brief, with the operator in the loop.

KRISEVA TAS helps bid teams discover, parse, score, and review tender opportunities.

  • Discover / ingest Live GeM discovery, DefProc public discovery, and operator-assisted protected-document flows.
  • Parse Parses PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, and CSV tender files locally.
  • Extract Extracts key tender metadata such as buyer fields, dates, values, EMD/ePBG, eligibility signals, and document requirements.
  • Compliance Extracts and structures compliance requirements for human review.
  • Decision Generates operator review briefs with BID, REVIEW, or SKIP recommendations.
